## 2. You do the answering. LiveCopilot helps you remember.

The hardest moments in an online interview or defense aren't always the questions you don't know.

Sometimes it's a project you know well but can't immediately explain. A number you prepared but can't recall. Or an answer buried somewhere in dozens of pages you can't search through in the middle of a conversation.

LiveCopilot doesn't answer for you. It brings back the things you've **already learned, already done, and already prepared** when you need them most.

---

## 3. Not just AI answers. Answers grounded in your own materials.

A general-purpose AI can answer questions, but it doesn't know your background.

Before an interview or meeting, you can import your **resume, project materials, papers, defense notes, job descriptions, and meeting documents** into LiveCopilot.

When a question comes up, LiveCopilot finds the most relevant information in those materials and combines it with the current conversation to help you shape your response.

### No digging through documents mid-conversation. Keep your answer moving.

Supports PDF, DOCX, Markdown, and TXT. Prepare once, retrieve when it matters.

---

## 4. Prepare beforehand. Let LiveCopilot handle the rest.

### 01 Import your materials

Add your resume, project documents, papers, and other important reference materials before the conversation begins.

### 02 Start the conversation

Turn on listening and LiveCopilot follows the conversation in real time. You can also type a question manually at any time.

### 03 Get a prompt

When a question comes up, relevant material and conversational context are turned into a clear response you can reference in the moment.

---

## 5. Not just for interviews. Use it whenever the conversation matters.

### Online Interviews

Load your resume, job description, and project materials beforehand. When questions turn to your experience, project details, or unexpected follow-ups, quickly bring the information you've prepared back into view.

### Research & Project Defenses

Import your paper, experimental results, and defense materials. When you need a number, method, or supporting argument, you don't have to search through dozens of pages during the discussion.

### Remote Meetings

Bring in project materials, product documentation, and meeting notes. When the discussion gets specific, quickly recover the context and details you need.

---

## 6. Your materials can stay on your Mac.

LiveCopilot can build and search your personal knowledge base locally. When generating a response, only the **current question, necessary conversational context, and relevant retrieved content** need to be sent to the AI service you choose — not your entire document library.

There is no LiveCopilot account and no developer-operated relay server. Your API key is stored securely in macOS Keychain.

**Your full knowledge base stays local. Only the context needed for the current response is used.**
