# LiveCopilot 2.2.0

- **混合知识回答**：默认结合你的资料与模型通用知识回答，也可切换为「仅知识库」。引用只展示实际采用的资料。
- **中英文自由切换**：回答语言默认 Auto，跟随当前问题；悬浮窗可直接选择中文或 English，支持跨语言使用知识库。
- **更及时的自动建议**：改进连续提问和「讲讲」「告诉我」等表达的识别，优化触发与首次检索等待；新增首字耗时和生成状态提示。
- **更清晰的模型选择**：提供语音识别选型提示，以及来自 Artificial Analysis、OpenRouter 的首 Token 延迟和生成速度参考。OpenAI 默认模型更新为 GPT-6.1 Sol，支持推理档位的预设在切换时优先选择 low。
- **共享时保护悬浮窗**：已通过腾讯会议整个屏幕共享与本地录制验证，对方画面隐藏悬浮窗，本机仍可正常查看和输入；同时保护已验证的苹果拼音候选条，输入时保持箭头鼠标。
- **更顺手的操作**：语言和知识模式使用一级菜单，统一设置页样式，并支持自定义悬浮窗显示快捷键。

## English

- **Hybrid knowledge** combines your documents with general model knowledge by default. Knowledge Base Only remains available, with citations limited to documents actually used.
- **Flexible answer language** follows each question in Auto mode. Choose 中文 or English directly in the overlay, including answers based on documents in another language.
- **Faster automatic suggestions** better recognize consecutive questions and requests such as “explain” or “tell me,” with improved triggering, retrieval preparation, and visible first-answer timing.
- **Clearer model selection** adds speech-model guidance and latency/throughput references from Artificial Analysis and OpenRouter. OpenAI now defaults to GPT-6.1 Sol; supported reasoning presets start at low when selected.
- **Overlay sharing protection** has been verified with Tencent Meeting full-screen sharing and local recording: the overlay remains usable locally while hidden from the shared image. Verified Apple Pinyin candidates are protected, and text entry keeps the arrow pointer.
- **Simpler controls** offer single-level language and knowledge menus, consistent settings pages, and a customizable overlay shortcut.
